# 🌟 LuxeStyle | Premium Fashion Store

> A sophisticated and fully responsive e-commerce web template for fashion (Men, Women, Baby), carefully crafted with an elegant Brown & Beige color palette and smooth 3D hover effects.

---

## 📸 Project Overview
LuxeStyle is a professional e-commerce store template inspired by high-end ThemeForest designs. It features stunning layouts, fluid transitions, and a modern aesthetic designed to provide a unique online shopping experience.

---

## ✨ Features
- 🎨 Luxury Color Palette: Harmonious shades of rich brown, warm beige, and gold accents that exude warmth and elegance.
- 🔄 Advanced Animations & 3D Flip Effects: Interactive and engaging visual transitions when hovering over products and cards.
- 🛍️ Comprehensive Sections:
  - Header & Navigation: Interactive top bar, dropdown menus, and a shopping cart badge.
  - Hero Section: Captivating banner introducing the latest seasonal collections.
  - Categories Section: Distinct categories for Men, Women, and Baby fashion with high-quality imagery.
  - Trending & Products Grid: Showcase of top items equipped with interactive action icons (Cart, Wishlist, Quick View).
  - Testimonials: Warm and cozy customer reviews slider.
  - Latest News: Mini-blog section for fashion tips and trend analysis.
  - Features / Delivery Icons: Fast delivery and secure payment highlights featuring unique hover animations.
  - Footer: Complete footer including a newsletter subscription box and quick links.

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used
- HTML5 (Structure)
- CSS3 (Styling, Custom Properties, Transitions, and Animations)
- Font Awesome (Icons)
- Google Fonts (Jost, Merriweather, and Clicker Script)
